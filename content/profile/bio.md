## Who I am

I'm Sai Srikar Chowdary Pattipati, a Computer Science Master's student at the
University of Massachusetts Boston (GPA 3.9/4.0, graduating May 2026),
specializing in Machine Learning and Natural Language Processing. I'm based in
Boston, MA, and I build AI systems end to end — from fine-tuning transformer
models to shipping full-stack LLM products with evaluation pipelines.

## What I do

Three things define how I work:

**I build real AI products, not demos.** My job-search platform pairs an LLM
evaluation pipeline with deterministic rendering guardrails; my portfolio is
itself a RAG system with source citations. I care about what happens after the
model call — evaluation, groundedness, failure handling.

**I understand the full stack.** I've fine-tuned DistilBERT to 91.8% accuracy
on a four-class mental-health classification task, and I've also built a
three-broker Kafka pipeline ingesting 5,000 events per second. AI engineering
needs both the modeling side and the systems side.

**I teach what I practice.** As a Teaching Fellow at UMass Boston I've taught
55 students across two semesters, including hands-on LLM engineering —
LangChain, RAG, agents, and workflow automation. Teaching a concept to
beginners is the strongest test of whether you actually understand it.

## What I'm looking for

I'm targeting Generative AI and LLM Engineering roles — building, evaluating,
and deploying LLM-powered systems in production. I'm open to any location and
to remote work.
