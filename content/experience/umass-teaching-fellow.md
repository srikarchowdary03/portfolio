## Role

Teaching Fellow / Instructor at the University of Massachusetts Boston,
September 2025 – May 2026, for two courses: IT110 (Fall 2025) and CS188sl
(Spring 2026) — 55 students total across two semesters.

## What the role involved

- Designed and delivered curriculum covering Python, Java OOP, REST APIs,
  algorithms, CS theory, and AI tooling.
- Introduced 25 students to LLM engineering: LangChain with RAG and agents,
  plus n8n workflow automation, taught through hands-on demos that bridge
  classroom instruction with current industry practice.
- Taught software-engineering practice to 25 spring students across 2
  sections: Java OOP, REST APIs, code reviews via Git/GitHub pull requests,
  and SQL-injection awareness.

## Why it matters for an AI engineering role

Teaching RAG and agent architectures to beginners requires understanding them
deeply enough to explain every moving part — the same skill needed to defend
design decisions in code review or explain an AI system to non-technical
stakeholders. It also demonstrates communication, curriculum design, and the
ability to stay current with LLM tooling while translating it for newcomers.
